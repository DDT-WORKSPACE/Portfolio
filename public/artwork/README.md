# Industry phone illustration

Generated with the built-in image generation tool for the DevsDen Tech industry-card redesign. `industry-phone.png` is the original output; `industry-phone.webp` is the optimized 800px transparent website asset. The phone is illustrative artwork, not a product screenshot or evidence of a delivered client project.

Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium technology studio website industry-card illustration. Create one exquisite 3D sculptural smartphone floating diagonally in a three-quarter view, screen facing front, white ceramic and brushed-silver frame, cobalt blue glass screen with minimal abstract white dashboard bars and an orange sphere, a translucent blue orbital ring wrapping behind the phone and two small orange spheres. Rounded polished product render, contemporary playful editorial 3D, physically realistic materials and studio illumination, restrained not toy-like. Palette white, cool light blue, vivid #1A62FF and small #FF6A00 accents. Single centered composition occupying 75% of a square canvas with ample transparent padding; real transparent background, soft contact shadow only. No text, no letters, no logos, no gradients in background, no dark background. This illustration must feel like a bespoke high-end digital-product sculpture, legible at 300px.

Project logos in `/public/brands` were cropped from the supplied portfolio artwork with `scripts/prepare-brand-assets.mjs`. They are original supplied wordmarks, not generated client identities.
